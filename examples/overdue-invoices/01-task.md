# 01 The task

> Fictitious example. The company, the people and all numbers are invented
> to show the way of working; they describe no real client.

**Company:** Beispiel Services GmbH, a service company with 14 people.
**Who brings the task:** Mara, who runs accounting part-time.

> "Every Monday I spend about three hours on overdue invoices. I export the
> open items from our accounting software, go through them one by one,
> decide who gets a reminder and in which tone, write the emails, and keep
> a list of the ones I must not chase because the customer has a complaint
> open. I make mistakes when I am in a hurry, and I am in a hurry every
> Monday."

**What she wants:** the Monday list prepared and the reminder emails
drafted, so she only reads, adjusts and sends.

**What she does not want:** anything sent automatically, and any reminder to
a customer who has a complaint open.

**Effort today:** about 3 hours a week, roughly 150 hours a year.
